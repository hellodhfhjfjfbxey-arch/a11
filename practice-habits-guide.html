Practice Habits That Stick

Motivation comes and goes. Habits do the heavy lifting. These five ideas make regular practice feel easy enough to keep doing.

  1. 1Make sessions small
  2. 2Attach practice to a cue
  3. 3Track your streak

0 of 5 lessons done
Lesson 1

Make sessions small

Ten or fifteen minutes done most days beats one long session a week. Small sessions are easy to start, and starting is the hardest part. On good days you will often keep going for longer.

Lesson 2

Attach practice to a cue

Link practice to something you already do every day: after morning coffee, before dinner, straight after work. Keep your materials out and ready so starting takes seconds, not minutes.

Lesson 3

Track your streak

A simple record keeps you honest and shows progress you might not notice.

  • Tick a calendar or a notebook after each session.
  • Note one thing you improved.
  • Never miss twice: one missed day is normal, two starts a new habit.
Lesson 4

Rest and sleep

Much of learning settles while you rest. Spread practice across the week rather than cramming, take short breaks within sessions, and protect your sleep. Coming back fresh often makes yesterday's struggle feel easier.

Lesson 5

Notice and enjoy progress

Every few weeks, look back at an early recording, drawing or project. Celebrate small wins, share what you have made, and set the next small goal. Enjoying the process is what keeps you practising for years.
